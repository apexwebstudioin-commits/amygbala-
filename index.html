FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about studying MBBS abroad.

What documents are required for MBBS abroad?
Typically, you'll need your 10+2 marksheet, NEET scorecard, passport, birth certificate, and medical fitness certificate. We'll guide you through the complete list.
How much does MBBS abroad cost?
Tuition fees vary by country and university, typically ranging from $3,000 to $12,000 per year. We provide transparent fee breakdowns for each university.
Which countries are suitable for Indian students?
Popular destinations include Russia, Georgia, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Poland, Hungary, and more. We help you choose based on your budget and preferences.
Do you provide visa assistance?
Yes, we provide complete visa guidance, including document preparation, application support, and interview preparation.
What is included in your service fee?
Our service fee covers university selection, application processing, documentation support, visa guidance, and pre-departure assistance. We keep it transparent and upfront.
