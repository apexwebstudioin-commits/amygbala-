FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries about studying MBBS abroad.

What documents are required for MBBS abroad?
Typically, you will need your 10th and 12th marksheets, NEET scorecard, valid passport, passport photos, and a medical fitness certificate. We will assist you with the checklist.
How much does MBBS abroad cost?
Tuition fees vary by country and university, typically ranging from $3,000 to $8,000 per year. We provide exact fee structures upfront.
Which countries are popular for Indian students?
Russia, Georgia, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, and Poland are among the most sought-after destinations due to NMC compliance and affordable budgets.
Do you provide full visa assistance?
Yes, we provide end-to-end visa guidance, including document apostille, translation, embassy submission, and pre-departure briefings.
